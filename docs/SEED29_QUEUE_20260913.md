## Exact handoff

1. A separate persistent CPU-only observer reads the current Stage-2 status
   every 30 seconds. It never signals processes, starts burns, writes a stop
   flag or touches current model/data/output files while waiting.
2. Require the full Stage-2 completion marker, including all five training
   arms, their dev evaluations, comparisons, final panel gate and burns.
   The last training arm finishing alone is not sufficient.
3. Observe the same verified original all-eight-GPU burn worker group for at
   least 180 seconds, sampling every 30 seconds. A changed worker group resets
   the window; a failed ownership/health check aborts without taking over.
4. Verify the predecessor's checkpoint/result identities and the seed-29 data,
   source-code and local Base-asset contracts while old burns remain active.
   The old observer remains the sole burn restarter during this phase.
5. Write the Stage-2 root's `STOP_IDLE_WATCH` and wait for the exact
   `scripts/pilot_stage2.py` observer to exit voluntarily. Recheck its tmux pane
   is no longer live. Never kill the observer, its tmux server or any launcher.
6. Only now reclaim verified burn-worker pidfds. Wait 30s/check all eight
   GPUs free, then repeat before starting seed-29 training.
7. Train the fresh seed-29 common model; require exit zero, wait 30s/check
   free and verify final model/optimizer/log/budget. Restore the original
   communicating `/tmp/llm_pretrain_burn.py` on all eight GPUs; verify it.
8. Write seed-29 `complete.json` and continue its idle-burn observer. Future
   jobs must disarm this new root's `STOP_IDLE_WATCH` before reclaiming GPUs.

A stale/failed predecessor or missing results stops the queue without altering
the predecessor's workload/burn recovery. After takeover, recovery refuses live
children or unknown GPU workloads. No automatic training retry or deletion.

## Training contract

- Same pinned local Qwen3-0.6B-Base config/tokenizer and 12-layer architecture.
- Fresh initialization, seed 29; data-order seed 1029. No seed-17 weights or
  optimizer state. No pretrained model weights and no memory reader/table.
- Same immutable English corpus and global 262144 non-padding tokens/update.
- Exactly 15259 updates / 4000055296 input tokens; original 3e-4 peak LR,
  306-step warmup and cosine to 3e-5. Same AdamW fp32 masters/moments and
  decay groups, bf16, eight ranks, microbatch 8, loss chunk 1024, non-reentrant
  activation checkpointing. The trainer uses torchrun, not Accelerate.
- Save every 1000 updates plus final checkpoint-15259; fresh output only.
  Require 100 GiB free before starting; existing files are never cleaned.
- `train_env` is explicitly activated inside the persistent tmux shell.
  Everything runs offline. No scientific-core or existing handoff file changes.
